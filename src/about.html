本地数据处理 / Local processing

Bilingo 读取当前 Bilibili 播放器已显示的字幕，用于本地分词、拼音和词语释义。字幕、视频地址和查词内容不会上传。Bilingo reads captions already displayed by the current player for local segmentation, pinyin and lookup. Caption text, video URLs and lookups are not uploaded.

权限 / Permission

  • storage：只保存启用状态和显示偏好 / stores activation and display preferences only.
  • bilibili.com/video：只在 Bilibili 视频页显示学习字幕 / runs the learning overlay only on Bilibili video pages.

不读取 Cookie、密码、账号标识或浏览历史；不下载视频；没有后端和分析服务。No cookies, credentials, account identifiers or browsing history are read; video is not downloaded; there is no backend or analytics.

删除 / Deletion

设置可在选项页重置，卸载扩展会删除扩展本地数据。Reset preferences from Settings or uninstall the extension to remove its local data.

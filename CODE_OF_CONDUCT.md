# Code of conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully and focus feedback on the work.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct issues privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub
profile or the repository's private reporting channel. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.
